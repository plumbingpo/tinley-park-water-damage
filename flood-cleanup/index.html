Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Tinley Park Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Tinley Park and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power

Why Flood Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
