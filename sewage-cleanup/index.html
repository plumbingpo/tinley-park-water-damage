Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. When it happens in Tinley Park, Tinley Park Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them

First, get everyone, animals included, away from the contaminated rooms. Do not flush, run faucets or start the washer or dishwasher, because added water feeds the backup. Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
